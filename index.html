Today's design: Cozy Cocoa Dots

Level 1 of 3About 25 min with drying Warm beige Chocolate brown

You need: base coat, beige polish, brown polish, top coat, and a toothpick or dotting tool.

  1. Apply base coat and let it dry for 2 minutes.
  2. Brush on two thin coats of beige, waiting 3 minutes between coats.
  3. Dip the toothpick lightly in brown and place three small dots down each nail. Let them dry.
  4. Seal everything with a glossy top coat.
Tip: wipe the toothpick before every dot so the dots stay tiny and neat.
